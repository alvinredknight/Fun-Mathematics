Slope = acceleration

On a velocity-time graph, the steepness of the line shows how quickly velocity changes. That rate of change is acceleration.

a = ΔvΔt
Δv: the rise (change in velocity) Δt: the run (change in time)

Try it: move the sliders

Area under the line = distance

Velocity × time gives distance. On the graph, that is the area between the line and the time axis. Split the area into rectangles and triangles, then add them up.

Rectangle: velocity × time Triangle: ½ × time × velocity

Build a trip

Find the area of each shaded shape, then the total distance travelled.
